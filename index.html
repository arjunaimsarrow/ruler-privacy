Ruler — Privacy Policy

Ruler · Version 1.4
By Arjun's Arrow
Privacy policy updated 24 September 2026.

Your measurements stay on your phone

Ruler works offline. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking. The app does not collect or transmit personal data to Arjun's Arrow or share it with third parties.

What the app uses locally

Ruler reads the phone's display density to set its scale. Where available, tilt events move the decorative leaves. Touch movements control the ruler and optional synthesised leaf sounds. These inputs are processed on your device and are not recorded or transmitted. Sound is generated by the app; it does not use your microphone.

Saved preferences

Your sound and leaf-motion preferences and any optional scale correction are stored only in the app's local storage. The app excludes its data from Android backup and transfer. Clear Ruler's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app to remove these preferences. Ruler does not maintain a measurement history.

Permissions and services

Ruler requests no Internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage access. It does not contact any server. Google Play and Android operate under their own privacy policies.

Accuracy

You can measure immediately using the phone's reported screen density. Accuracy depends on the device. An optional bank-card adjustment is available if needed. Ruler is not intended for safety-critical precision measurements.

Contact

For support or privacy questions: arjunaimsarrow@gmail.com. If you email us, we use the information you choose to provide to respond to your request. Do not include sensitive information unnecessarily.
